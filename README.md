## 🎮 Project Overview

**النسر الدامي** is a large-scale multiplayer online strategy game that combines:
- **Islamic-Andalusian** architectural aesthetics with **Mongolian** design elements
- Epic orchestral music blending Eastern and Western instruments
- Complex alliance and diplomacy systems
- Revolutionary combat mechanics with revival cycles

## 🎨 Art Direction & Visual Identity

### Architecture Style
- **Islamic-Andalusian** with **Mongolian** fusion
- Dark atmospheric color palette
- Heavy, tactile materials (basalt stone, oxidized bronze)

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Dark Gray (#2D2D2D), Black (#000000)
- **Accent**: Blood Orange (#FF4500), Dark Gold (#B8860B)
- **Atmosphere**: Epic, dark, haunting

### Materials
- Dark basalt stone
- Oxidized copper/bronze
- Heavy ebony wood
- Glowing red sources (blood, lava, mystical energy)

## 🎵 Audio Design

### Musical Style
- **Epic Orchestral** with Eastern instruments (oud, qanun) + Western brass
- **Dynamic tempo** based on player state and scene
- **Heavy tactile SFX** for combat and building

### Scene-Specific Music
- **Castle View**: Peaceful, strategic contemplation
- **World Map**: Triumphant, world-conquering
- **Combat**: Intense, percussive, battle-focused

## 🏛️ Game Systems

### Combat System
- **3-Phase Battles**: Initial Exchange → Mid-Battle → Final Struggle
- **Revival Cycles**: Units can return in future cycles
- **Asymmetric Warfare**: Different unit types with unique roles

### Alliance System
- **Multi-Tier Structure**: Leader → Commander → Officer → Member
- **Diplomatic Tools**: Trade, treaties, shared territory
- **Alliance Wars**: Large-scale territorial conflicts

### Economic Buildings
- **Resource Production**: Farms, quarries, mines
- **Research Buildings**: Library of Wisdom, Observatory
- **Support Structures**: Hospitals, alliance halls, secret trade

### World Map
- **Hexagonal Grid**: Strategic movement and positioning
- **Terrain Types**: Grasslands, mountains, water, fog of war
- **Strategic Points**: Throne Palace, Guardian Towers, Divine Resources
